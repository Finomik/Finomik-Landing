About us

We teach how to decide.

Finomik is a financial education platform for schools. We exist so that no young person reaches their first salary, first contract or first investment without knowing what they're deciding.

Our story

It started in a classroom.
Today it is for the whole world.

→01 · The discovery

The United States

Joaquino and Miquel went to study in the United States and found something that changed how they saw things: classmates their age talked naturally about saving, investing, credit and taxes. It wasn't talent, it was education. They had learned it from a young age.

→02 · The question

Spain

Back home, the gap was impossible to ignore. We asked friends, families and teachers, and the answer was always the same: nobody had explained it to them. Young people reached their first salary, first contract or first loan without knowing what they were signing.

●03 · The mission

The world

We wanted to change it at home. But the further we went, the clearer it became: the problem wasn't local or national, it was global. That's why Finomik was born for the world.

Mission and vision

Mission

To give every young person, wherever they are, the judgement to make their own financial decisions.

Vision

For financial education to become the standard, not the exception.
